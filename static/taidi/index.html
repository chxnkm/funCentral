    Tai Di · Big Two

    How to Play

    Be the first to get rid of every card in your hand. 2–4 players, one deck.

    Card order

    Ranks, low to high — the 2 is the highest card:

    345678910JQKA2

    Suits break ties between equal ranks, low to high:

    ♦ Diamonds♣ Clubs♥ Hearts♠ Spades

    Plays

    Single, pair or triple of the same rank, or a five-card hand. Five-card hands, weakest to strongest:

    1. StraightFive in a row. 3-4-5-6-7 up to 10-J-Q-K-A; never wraps, no 2s.
    2. FlushFive of one suit. Suit decides first, then the top card.
    3. Full houseA triple and a pair. Goes by the triple.
    4. Four of a kindFour of a rank plus any card. Goes by the four.
    5. Straight flushA straight in one suit — the strongest hand.

    Turns

    1. Whoever holds the lowest card (the 3♦) opens, and the first play must include it.
    2. Each player then plays the same number of cards that beat the table, or passes. Any stronger five-card hand beats a weaker one.
    3. Passing doesn't lock you out — you can play again on your next turn.
    4. When everyone else passes, the last player to play takes the table and leads anything they like.

    Fewer than four players

    PlayersDeal

    Winning & rating

    The first player to empty their hand wins. Everyone else is ranked by how few cards they have left. Each game is rated like a match against every other player at the table, so beating stronger players earns more.

    Table tips

    • Slide a finger across your cards to pick several at once.
    • 💡 Hint cycles through every legal play, weakest first.
    • Sort switches your hand between rank and suit order.
    • You can line up your next play while others take their turn.
